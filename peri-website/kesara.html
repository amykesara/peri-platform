About

I hold a Master of Music in Cello Performance from the Royal College of Music, London (2023–2025) and a Bachelor of Music from the San Francisco Conservatory of Music (2019–2023), where I also served as a peer mentor to first-year students. I grew up in Bangkok, attending Harrow International School, and am now back home and thrilled to teach.

I have over six years of teaching experience with students of all ages — from young beginners through to pre-conservatory level. My approach blends ABRSM graded technique with Suzuki-inspired musicality, always aiming to make the cello feel joyful rather than intimidating.
