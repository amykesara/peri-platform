Lessons & booking
Browse tutors, click on a profile you like, and use the booking panel on the right to choose a day, time slot, lesson length (30, 60, or 90 minutes), and format (online or in-person where available). You'll see a full fee breakdown before confirming.
Yes — lessons can be rescheduled or cancelled up to 24 hours before the start time at no charge. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a fee at the tutor's discretion. We'll always show you the tutor's cancellation policy on their profile page.
You'll receive a confirmation with the Zoom link (or meeting details) for your lesson. Your tutor will also reach out before your first session to introduce themselves and ask about your goals. If you have any issues, our support team is reachable at hello@perimusic.co.th.
Most tutors on Peri teach using a structured curriculum and will provide practice notes, sheet music, or exercises after each session. Ask your tutor during the intro call what their approach looks like — you'll quickly get a sense of their teaching style.
Pricing & payment
Each tutor sets their own hourly rate — typically between ฿400 and ฿1,200/hour depending on experience and instrument. Peri may add a transparent platform fee at checkout, and you'll always see the complete price breakdown before confirming.
We accept PromptPay, major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and bank transfer. Payment is processed securely at the time of booking.
Some tutors may offer multi-lesson packages at a discounted rate — you'll see this noted on their profile. Peri is still refining the best pricing model for regular students.
For tutors
You set your own rate freely, and Peri offers pricing guidance based on your experience and instrument. Students see a clear price breakdown before booking. We are still refining the final payment model as the platform develops.
