About

I graduated from the Mahidol College of Music with a degree in classical piano performance, and have been teaching privately for 9 years. My students range from complete beginners — children as young as 5 and adults returning to music — through to diploma level.

I believe every student learns differently, so I tailor each lesson to what works for you. I'm patient, encouraging, and genuinely enjoy seeing students discover what music can do for them.

I teach classical repertoire, jazz harmony and improvisation, and contemporary pop. I also prepare students for ABRSM and Trinity examinations.
