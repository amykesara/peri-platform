Our story

Music education in Thailand
deserves to be better.

Peri was built on a simple belief: the best music teachers shouldn't have to fight algorithms to find students — and serious students shouldn't have to settle for whoever shows up first in a search.

What we believe

Built on three principles

01
Curation over volume
Every tutor on Peri has been personally reviewed — credentials checked, a trial lesson observed, and ongoing student feedback monitored. We'd rather have 50 exceptional teachers than 5,000 average ones.
02
Fair pay for educators
Tutors set their own rates with guidance from Peri based on experience, instrument, and student level. Students see a clear price breakdown before booking.
03
Progress you can see
Lessons without structure fade. Peri students follow clear learning paths, hit real milestones, and earn certificates they can be proud of. Every teacher builds a curriculum, not just a booking calendar.

The story

Why we started Peri

Thailand has an extraordinary depth of musical talent — conservatory-trained pianists, classically schooled violinists, self-taught guitarists who've played for decades. But finding them, vetting them, and booking them was always a patchwork of LINE messages, Facebook groups, and word of mouth.

We built Peri to change that. A curated platform where quality comes first: where students can browse verified profiles, request a short intro call, and book lessons with a tutor who fits their goals.

We launched online-first so that students anywhere in Thailand — not just Bangkok — can access the same quality of instruction. Your location shouldn't limit your musical education.
