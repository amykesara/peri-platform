Where do you sit on the scale?

We use the classical dynamic markings — from pp to ff — to describe where you are in your musical journey. Find your level and discover what lessons will look like for you.

pp Beginner p Elementary mf Intermediate f Advanced ff Diploma
pp
Pianissimo
Complete beginner
Find a pp tutor →

You're just starting out.

You've never played before — or picked up an instrument years ago and let it go. Either way, you're starting from zero, and that's perfectly fine. Every great musician was once here. At pp, the goal isn't perfection; it's building a real foundation, falling in love with your instrument, and making your first sounds feel intentional.

What to expect from your lessons

🤝
Getting comfortable
Your tutor starts with posture, hand position, and how to hold or sit at your instrument correctly — the habits that make everything easier later.
🎵
Reading music basics
Notes, rhythm, time signatures — introduced gradually alongside your playing so theory never feels abstract or disconnected.
🎯
Your first real pieces
Simple, satisfying music you'll actually recognise and enjoy. The aim is for you to feel like a musician from lesson one.
🗓️
A structured path forward
Your tutor maps out a clear curriculum so you always know what you're working toward, with milestones that keep progress visible.
p
Piano
Some experience
Find a p tutor →

You know the basics.

You've had some lessons, followed a tutorial online, or taught yourself the fundamentals. You can play simple pieces but feel like there are gaps — things you're doing by habit rather than understanding. At p level, lessons focus on closing those gaps, building technique more deliberately, and expanding your repertoire in a direction that actually excites you.

What to expect from your lessons

🔧
Technique audit
Your tutor identifies what you've picked up well and what needs adjusting — catching bad habits early before they become harder to fix.
📖
Deeper music reading
Moving past basics into dynamics, articulation, and expression — learning to read music as a language, not just a set of instructions.
🎼
Repertoire that challenges
Pieces that stretch you without overwhelming — carefully chosen to build new skills while keeping lessons engaging and rewarding.
🧠
Understanding why
Less doing-what-you're-told, more understanding the music. Your tutor explains the reasoning behind technique so you can self-correct between sessions.
mf
Mezzo-forte
Intermediate
Find an mf tutor →

You're comfortable, but not quite there yet.

You can play confidently and tackle pieces of real substance. You have a feel for music — you've developed an ear, a sense of style. But you know there's a ceiling you haven't broken through, technically or expressively. At mf, lessons become more nuanced: refining sound, developing musical personality, and taking on repertoire that demands genuine artistry.

What to expect from your lessons

🎨
Musical expression
Moving beyond correct notes into real interpretation — colour, phrasing, timing, dynamics — developing your own voice on the instrument.
⚡
Technical refinement
Advanced exercises, scales, and études that build speed, evenness, and control — the kind of technical work that separates good from excellent.
🎭
Substantial repertoire
Longer, more demanding works that require weeks of preparation — pieces you'll be genuinely proud to perform for family, friends, or an audience.
👂
Ear training
Developing a more critical ear so you can hear what needs improving before your tutor says a word — a skill that accelerates all future progress.
f
Forte
Advanced
Find an f tutor →

You play at a performance level.

You're past the point where technique is the limiting factor. You've performed in public, perhaps competed or passed formal exams. What you're looking for now is refinement, depth, and a tutor who can match your level — someone who can hear the small things, challenge your interpretations, and push you in ways that general teachers can't. At f, lessons are conversations between serious musicians.

What to expect from your lessons

🏛️
Masterclass-style coaching
Deep, detailed work on a small number of pieces — the kind of focused refinement that live performance demands and that separates polished from professional.
🎤
Performance preparation
Recitals, concerts, competitions — your tutor helps you manage nerves, shape your programme, and perform at your best when it counts.
📚
Repertoire & style depth
Exploring the history and context behind music you're playing — understanding style periods and composers in a way that transforms interpretation.
🔬
Fine-grained feedback
At this level, your tutor catches micro-details — subtle tension, tiny rhythmic inconsistencies, voicing choices — that make the difference between good and memorable.
ff
Fortissimo
Diploma & exam prep
Find an ff tutor →

You're preparing for something significant.

ABRSM Grade 8, Trinity diplomas, conservatory auditions, university entrance — you have a specific, high-stakes target and you need a tutor who has been there themselves. At ff, every lesson is purposeful. The syllabus is known, the timeline is clear, and the work is intense. Your tutor has guided students through these exact exams and knows what panels and examiners are looking for.

What to expect from your lessons

📋
Exam-mapped curriculum
Every lesson planned against the syllabus requirements — pieces, scales, sight-reading, aural tests — so nothing is left to chance in the exam room.
⏱️
Mock exams & timed practice
Simulated exam conditions so the real thing feels familiar. Your tutor marks you using the actual assessment criteria and gives structured, written feedback.
🎓
Conservatory-level tutors
Every ff tutor on Peri holds advanced qualifications and has personally guided students through diploma and audition preparation successfully.
🧩
Holistic exam readiness
Not just the pieces — viva preparation, written paper support, and the mental and physical readiness to perform under pressure on the day.
